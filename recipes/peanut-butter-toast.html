Description

This is a delicious and basic peanut butter toast. There is nothing special about it. It doesn't take long to make either.

Ingredients

  • 1 slice of bread
  • Peanut butter

Steps

  1. Toast the bread
  2. Spread peanut butter on top
  3. Enjoy!
